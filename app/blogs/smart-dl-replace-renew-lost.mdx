The Smart DL is the card-format driving licence. Here is what NTSA charges to renew or replace it, and where to start in the four situations that send people to NTSA in a panic. DriveRush is not affiliated with NTSA, and fees change, so check the amount on the NTSA service portal before you pay.

## What NTSA charges

| Service | NTSA's fee | Time NTSA gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renew a Smart DL (three years) | KES 3,050 | 5 working days |
| Duplicate Smart DL (lost, defaced or change of name) | KES 3,050 | 5 working days |
| Add a class to your licence (endorsement) | KES 550 | |
| Update your driving licence record | Free | Immediate |

The renewal and duplicate fees are from NTSA's own service pages, last updated 14 May 2026. The record update is from NTSA's 2026 service charter, which also lists a one-year renewal at KES 650, but the renewal service itself offers only the three-year card. Read the fee on the portal before you pay.

All NTSA services run through [eCitizen](https://ecitizen.go.ke), then the NTSA service portal. The old TIMS system no longer exists, so ignore any advice that sends you there.

If you're applying for the first time (not replacing), the post you want is the [2026 NTSA walkthrough](/blogs/ntsa-driving-licence-kenya-2026). For reference, NTSA lists the first-time Smart DL application at KES 3,050 and 5 working days, collected at a Huduma centre or NTSA after an SMS ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/application-for-a-smart-driving-licence)).

## 1. The card is lost or stolen

1. **Report the loss to the police** and keep the record they give you. NTSA may ask for it.
2. **Log in to eCitizen,** open the NTSA service portal and choose the duplicate driving licence service.
3. **Choose the reason** (lost, defaced or change of name) and read the requirements.
4. **Pay KES 3,050 inside eCitizen** and keep the confirmation.

Then wait for the SMS before you go to collect anything.

## 2. The card is expiring or expired: how to renew

NTSA's renewal page says a licence can be renewed after a period of three years, and lists these steps:

1. **Log in to [eCitizen](https://ecitizen.go.ke)** and open the NTSA service portal.
2. **Choose driving licence renewal,** then "smart DL (3 years)".
3. **Check your details** and click renew.
4. **Pay KES 3,050** inside eCitizen and keep the confirmation.
5. **Wait for the SMS** that says your new card is ready, then collect it.

**If it has already expired,** renew it the same way, and don't drive until you have. Failing to renew a driving licence is a minor offence with a KES 1,000 penalty under the Traffic (Minor Offences) Rules 2016. We found no late-renewal surcharge in NTSA's own pages.

**Drivers aged 60 and over** still renew the same way. In December 2025 NTSA said a proposal for yearly renewal with a medical check at 60 was not adopted.

## 3. The card is damaged

A bent or cracked card usually means applying for a duplicate, the same KES 3,050 service as a lost card. If only the chip seems damaged, ask at an NTSA office what they advise before you start an application. Bring the card and your ID.

## 4. The details on the card are wrong

This is rare but it happens: a name spelt wrong, a wrong date of birth, a wrong photo or class. NTSA's charter lists updating a driving licence record as free, and a change of name goes through the duplicate service. If the card itself is wrong, take it and your ID to NTSA and ask how to correct it. Whether you pay may depend on whether the error was in your application or in the printing, so bring any receipts you have.

## After the new card

A fresh Smart DL is the right moment to check that the class on the card is the one you need. We covered that in [which licence class you need](/blogs/class-b-c-d-licence-kenya).

If it's been a while since you drove and you want to refresh the signs before you get back on the road, the [Pelican chart](/road-signs/pelican) and [3D signs](/road-signs/3d) cover 83 signs, free, no signup.

## When in doubt

The most useful habit with any bureaucracy: take screenshots at every step. Receipt screen, payment confirmation, SMS, status page. When something goes sideways, the person who has the timestamps has the easier conversation.

Drive safe. Renew on time.
