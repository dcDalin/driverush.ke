So expect anything from about KSh 7,500 to KSh 25,000 for a regular car course, and up to KSh 50,000 for a premium one with your own instructor. Most of these prices are tuition only. Prices change, and these may not cover everything, so confirm the total with the school.

AA Kenya says it has over 100 branches in major towns. For motorcycles, it lists A2 at KSh 5,780, and Kisumu National Polytechnic lists Class A at KSh 6,950 including NTSA charges.

**NTSA's fees are on top, unless a school's price includes them.** They come to [KES 5,500 for a first licence](/ntsa-test): KES 650 provisional, KES 1,050 test booking, KES 750 interim and KES 3,050 Smart DL. Kisumu National Polytechnic notes that students pay NTSA charges themselves through eCitizen, not to the school. Ask which of these your quote covers.

## How to check a driving school is licensed

- **Ask to see the licence.** NTSA's April 2026 notice says all schools must register, and that the licence must be printed and displayed on the premises. No licence on the wall is a reason to walk out.
- **Know the penalty is real.** The same report cites a fine of up to KSh 20,000, or imprisonment, for running a school without proper registration.
- **Ask NTSA if you're unsure.** NTSA says a list of licensed schools is available on its website, but we couldn't find it when we checked, so [contact NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/contact) directly.
- **Be careful with agents.** NTSA warned in September 2026 about fraudsters posing as driving school agents and promising to fast-track licences.

## Questions to ask before you pay

1. Can I see your NTSA licence for this branch?
2. What's the total, in writing, and what does it include?
3. Are NTSA's fees included, or do I pay them separately through eCitizen?
4. How many lessons do I get, and how much of that is me behind the wheel?
5. Which licence class is this course for, and is it automatic or manual?
6. Will you help me book the test, and can I practise on a Model Town Board?
7. Do you teach the board's lane options, the minus 1 rule and the route rules the way the examiner at my test centre expects?
8. What happens, and what does it cost, if I need a retest?

## How long does driving school take?

Usually one to three months for a car, among the schools that publish a length. [Rocky](/driving-schools-kenya/rocky-driving-school) lists 1 month for each licence course, and [Prince](/driving-schools-kenya/prince-driving-school) 4 to 5 weeks. [Top Gear](/driving-schools-kenya/top-gear-driving-school) says 1 to 2 months, at 1 to 2 hours a day. [Wings](/driving-schools-kenya/wings-driving-school) says up to 3 months for a full course and 1 month for a half course. Meru University of Science and Technology lists 25 practical and theory lessons over 1 month for Class B.

NTSA's timing sets the shortest path: your PDL has to mature for 21 days before you can book the test, and it's valid for 3 months. So even a fast course takes at least three weeks from your PDL to your test.

## Make the most of your school

Your school teaches you to drive. The [NTSA test](/ntsa-test) also expects you to know the [Model Town Board](/model-town-board) and the road signs, and those take practice between lessons.

- **The Model Town Board.** Drive it on our free trainer, level by level. It follows the rules driving schools teach: each lane's options at the roundabout, the minus 1 rule for your exit lane, the shortest correct route first and parking only as a last option. [The board's rules](/model-town-board/rules) are all on one page. If your school teaches something differently, go with your school.
- **Road signs.** Learn them on the <Link to="/road-signs/pelican">Pelican chart</Link> or with <Link to="/road-signs/3d">3D signs</Link>.

All of these are free, with no signup, and work alongside your school, not instead of it.
